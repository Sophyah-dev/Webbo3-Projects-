# Project Documentation 

## Project Topic: Medicare Pharma Store September 2026 sales Data Analysis 

### Project Overview

Medicare Pharma Ltd. is a pharmaceutical company operating across five branches: Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan and Kano.

This project focuses on examining and preparing the company's September 2026 sales dataset to understand its structure, assess data quality and identify inconsistencies that could affect the reliability of subsequent analysis.

### Role
I worked as a Product Analyst, focusing on product records, product-name consistency and product-category classifications.


### Objectives

- Examine product records within the sales dataset.
- Identify unique product names and product categories.
- Identify spelling errors and inconsistencies in product names.
- Identify missing product-category entries.
- Examine inconsistencies in product-category naming and classification.
- Document identified discrepancies for further review.
