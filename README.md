# Project Documentation 

## Project Topic: Medicare Pharma Store sales Data Cleaning 

### Project Overview

This project focuses on exploring and preparing sales data for Medicare Pharma Ltd, a pharmaceutical company operating across five branches: Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan and Kano.

This project involves examining and preparing the company's September 2026 sales dataset to understand its structure, assess data quality and identify inconsistencies that could affect the reliability of subsequent analysis.

### Role
I worked as a Product Analyst, focusing on product records, product-name consistency and product-category classifications.


### Objectives

- Examine product records within the sales dataset.
- Identify unique product names and product categories.
- Identify spelling errors and inconsistencies in product names.
- Identify missing product-category entries.
- Examine inconsistencies in product-category naming and classification.
- Document identified discrepancies for further review.


### Dataset Description

The dataset contains pharmaceutical sales records for September 2026 across five branches of Medicare Pharma Ltd.
It consists of  162 records and 9 columns.


- Sale_ID : Unique identifier for each sales record

- Sale_Date : Date the sale occurred

- Branch : Location where the sale was recorded

- Product_Name : Name of the pharmaceutical product sold

- Product_Category : Category assigned to the product

- Quantity : Number of units sold

- Unit_Price : Price per unit of the product

- Customer_Type : Classification of the customer

- Payment_Method : Method used to complete the transaction



### Project Approach

The product assessment followed these steps:

1. Product Identification : 
Examined the dataset to identify unique product names and product category

2. Product Name Assessment : 
Reviewed product names to identify spelling errors, variations and inconsistencies.

3. Product Category Assessment : 
Examined product-category entries to identify missing values, inconsistent capitalization and variations in category naming.

4. Issue Documentation : 
Recorded identified discrepancies alongside their corresponding Sale IDs to maintain traceability.

5. Observation Reporting : 
Documented the identified issues to support the team's overall data quality assessment.



### Key Observations

The product assessment revealed the following data quality issues:

- Spelling inconsistencies in product names.

- Variations in product naming conventions.

- Inconsistent capitalization of product 
categories.

- Missing product-category entries.

- Inconsistent category naming conventions.


### Data Integrity and Limitations

The original dataset was not modified during my assessment. All identified inconsistencies and observations were documented separately for review.
The assessment was limited to product-related records and did not constitute a complete cleaning.
